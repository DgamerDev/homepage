    §2 What data is collected?

    §2.1 Server-Logs

    Server logs typically contain:
    - IP addresses
    - Date and time of the request
    - Amount of data transferred
    - Referrer
    - User agent
    All of this information is deleted after at least 3 months.

    §2.2 Cloudflare CDN

    This website uses the services of Cloudflare
    (Cloudflare Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA)
    as a CDN (Content Delivery Network).
    For technical reasons, Cloudflare processes connection data, including IP addresses and request metadata,
    in order to deliver the website, protect it from attacks, and collect analytics.

    - Purpose on our part: Protection of the website
    - Legal basis: Legitimate interest
    - Data transfer: Cloudflare may transfer data to the United States. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
    - Cloudflare Privacy Policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
